SECTION 1

Executive summary

Phase 1 gives Trend a complete, secure foundation and the everyday screens your team uses to run sales, purchasing and stock — all designed as one clean, modern system.

Think of Phase 1 in two halves. The first half is the foundation: secure sign-in, companies and branches, users and permissions, and the shared services (document numbering, activity history, search) that everything else relies on. The second half is the daily work: customers and suppliers, your item catalogue, and the sales, purchase and stock documents your staff create every day.

One part — the accounting engine that turns those documents into official financial records — is intentionally handled with extra care. We build the screens, but we do not switch on the automatic accounting until your accountant has confirmed the rules. This protects you: a wrong accounting rule is the most expensive kind of mistake to fix later, so we get it right before it goes live, not after.

SECTION 2

What Phase 1 means

Trend will grow into a large system over time. Rather than build everything at once, we deliver it in phases so you see real progress early and nothing is rushed. Phase 1 is the first working version — the foundation plus the core business screens.

Everything in Phase 1 is built to be reused. Because Trend is designed to serve many businesses, the foundation is multi-company and multi-branch from day one, works in English and Arabic, and handles US Dollar and Lebanese Pound side by side. Nothing is hard-wired to a single company.

A note on the prototype. Alongside this document you have an interactive prototype — a clickable preview of the real screens. It uses sample data and saves nothing; it is there so you can see and feel the product before a single line of the final system is written.

SECTION 4

Platform foundation

This is the invisible groundwork that makes Trend trustworthy. Most of it your staff never see directly, but everything depends on it.

  • Secure sign-in. Modern password protection, secure sessions, and the ability to set the exact hours a user is allowed to log in — a feature you specifically asked for.
  • Activity history. Every important action is recorded — who did what and when — so nothing is a mystery.
  • Document numbering. Clean, consistent, official numbers for every invoice and voucher, with the right reset rules.
  • Search & attachments. The groundwork so you can find records quickly and attach files such as an item photo.
  • Reliability & monitoring. The system watches itself, so problems are caught early.
Why foundation matters. A house is only as good as what is under it. By building this first, every screen we add later is automatically secure, consistent and reliable — instead of each part reinventing it.

SECTION 6

Company setup

Trend is multi-company from the start. You will be able to set up each of your eight companies, their branches, and their warehouses, and configure each company's own settings.

  • Companies & branches — each with its own details and users.
  • Warehouses & locations — the places your stock lives.
  • Per-company settings — currency, fiscal year, and whether VAT applies to that company (you can turn VAT on or off per company).
SECTION 7

Users & permissions

You decide exactly who can see and do what — and you can be very precise about it.

  • Users — add your staff and control their access.
  • Roles — group permissions into sensible roles such as Accountant, Sales, or Warehouse.
  • Fine-grained permissions — scoped by branch and warehouse, so a Tripoli salesperson sees Tripoli, and whether someone can see cost prices or not is fully under your control.

SECTION 11

Finance & Accounting module

This is the heart of your books, and the one we treat most carefully. Finance & Accounting is its own separate module — never mixed into Stock or Sales.

Finance & Accounting

  • Chart of Accounts
  • Linked Accounts — your per-company accounting routing, so each entry posts to the account you choose (not fixed in the software)
  • Statement of Account
  • The posting engine — the single, controlled place that writes to your books
Why this waits. Accounting rules must come from your accountant, not be guessed by software. Two things unlock this module: your real chart of accounts, and your accountant's written confirmation of the VAT and posting rules. Until then, we build the screens but never invent a number — a blank, honest screen is far safer than a plausible wrong one.
SECTION 12

Stock module

Full visibility of what you hold and every movement in and out — a separate module from Finance.

Stock

  • Inventory overview and item detail
  • Opening Stock, Stock In, Stock Out — kept as distinct transaction types
  • A unified Stock Movement screen for easy viewing
One screen, distinct workflows. You will see a single, convenient Stock Movement view — but behind it, Opening Stock, Stock In and Stock Out stay separate transaction types, so the data is always precise.

SECTION 15

What is deliberately deferred

To keep Phase 1 focused and safe, a few things are intentionally not in it. This is by design, not an oversight:

Automatic accounting entries — the screens are ready; the automatic posting waits for your accountant's confirmed rules.
Commercial plans & pricing — the subscription machinery is built; what the plans are and cost is your decision, later.
Offline / desktop application — planned for a later phase; Phase 1 prepares for it but does not include it.
Additional modules — POS, CRM, payroll, manufacturing and similar are future possibilities, not part of this phase.
